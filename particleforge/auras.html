Plugins / ParticleForge

Worn auras

Since 1.7.0 an aura is an effect a player wears: whoever has its permission carries it while online. It follows the player, restarts on its own when it ends and hides in spectator mode or while the player is invisible. Use it for rank or subscription perks.

# config.yml
auras:
  - id: dragon
    effect: cosmetics/dragon-aura          # any effect from /pf list
    permission: particleforge.aura.dragon
  • First matching entry wins: list the most exclusive aura first.

  • Aura permissions are nobody's by default, not even ops'. Give them with your permissions plugin: /lp user <player> permission set particleforge.aura.dragon true (or to a group).

  • /pf reload re-reads the list.

  • Bundled example: cosmetics/dragon-aura (violet flame rising around the player, gold sparks at the feet). On a server that already has an effects/ folder, copy effects/cosmetics/dragon-aura.yml from the jar: bundled effects are only extracted into an empty folder.

  • Every aura counts against the per-player effect budget (budget.per-player-max-active-effects).
